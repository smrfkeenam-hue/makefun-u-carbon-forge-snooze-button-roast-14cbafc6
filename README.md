One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning, entered in the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition "Snooze Button Roast".

## The entry

> Saturday, 6 AM: my alarm clock rings, not to wake me, but to get a front-row seat while I lose an argument with a button.

The joke flips the alarm clock from helper to spectator: it does not ring to wake anyone up, it rings to watch its owner lose a fight with the snooze button. The punchline lands in the last few words, and the whole line is sayable in one breath.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
